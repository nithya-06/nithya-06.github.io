About

Pipelines that stay up, services that stay fast

I started out writing Java and Spring Boot microservices for media measurement at Nielsen, then moved deeper into data — Spark, Scala and streaming ingestion at scale. Today I work on Apple's Insights team, designing ETL pipelines and the metrics behind the dashboards teams use to make product decisions.

The through-line is the same either way: understand where the data actually comes from, make the slow part fast, and leave behind something the next engineer can read. Lately that's also meant building LLM-backed services — retrieval pipelines, embeddings and vector search, and multi-agent workflows with LangGraph — on the same platform foundations.

Outside of the pipeline work I'm usually reading about distributed systems design or breaking something in a side project to see how it fails.

About

Services that stay fast under load

I spent my first three years writing Spring Boot microservices for media analytics at Nielsen — multithreaded ingestion, billions of records a month, and the unglamorous work of tracking down concurrency bugs in production. That's where I learned how distributed systems actually break.

At Apple I still write backend services, but a growing share of my work is AI infrastructure: a multi-agent service built on FastAPI and LangGraph that turns natural-language questions into charts and generated Python widgets, RAG pipelines with embeddings and vector search, and the streaming, caching and observability layers that make all of it survive a load balancer.

The instinct is the same in both halves of the job — find the slow part, measure it, and leave behind something the next engineer can read.
